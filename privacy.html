DANYX Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

DANYX ("we", "our", or "the application") is a social media analysis application designed to work with publicly available information from supported social media platforms.

1. Information We Access

DANYX may access publicly available information provided through supported platform APIs, such as public video information, public comments, engagement statistics, usernames, publication dates, and other information made publicly available by the respective platform.

2. Use of Information

Information accessed through the application is used for analysis, organization, visualization, and research of publicly available social media content.

3. Personal Information

DANYX does not intentionally request passwords or private account credentials from social media users. The application is designed to work with information made publicly available through supported platform APIs.

4. Data Storage

Information may be temporarily processed or stored locally by the application for analysis purposes. DANYX does not sell publicly accessed information to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

DANYX may use official APIs and services provided by third-party platforms. Their respective privacy policies and terms may also apply when their services are used.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the DANYX application administrator through the official contact method associated with the application.
